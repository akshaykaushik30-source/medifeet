## 5. What the products promise

Feature tags used across the catalogue (count of products):
cushioned 183 · arch support 179 · lightweight 163 · orthopedic 137 ·
slip-resistant 136 · anatomic 128 · breathable 38 · vegan-friendly 15.

The four bullet points on almost every product:
1. Support from Feet to Spine
2. Technology Driven Easy Walking
3. Cushioned Footbed with Arch Support
4. Lightweight, Comfy and Durable

Other recurring claims: Diabetes & Ortho-Friendly · Anti-skid sole for firm grip ·
Bounce-back footbed for tireless walking · Handcrafted with genuine leather ·
In collaboration with BASF.

The four headline features (already on the new site): Anatomic Design ·
Advanced Cushioning · Anti-Skid Sole · Bounce-Back.

Health wording to handle carefully: product copy says "pain relief", "pain free
walking" and "diabetes friendly". These are marketing claims; keep them as
comfort and support benefits, not medical promises, unless the team has
certification to back them.
